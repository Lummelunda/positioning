# Website positioning analysis: Vizgard

**Analysed:** 27 September 2026  
**Scope:** Publicly communicated positioning, based on Vizgard's website. This does not independently verify product claims or establish how buyers actually perceive the company.

## Source coverage

Reviewed the [homepage](https://vizgard.com/) and its linked pages for [FortifAI](https://vizgard.com/product/fortifai/), [UnifAI](https://vizgard.com/product/unifai/) and [VizEdge](https://vizgard.com/product/vizedge/).

## Analyst synthesis

Vizgard appears to position itself as a **UK sovereign visual autonomy supplier for defence and security organisations** that want existing cameras and uncrewed systems to detect, interpret and respond to threats with less continuous operator attention. FortifAI is the operational centre of the offer; UnifAI extends it into model and fleet operations, while VizEdge supplies a field-deployable hardware option. This is a synthesis of the [homepage](https://vizgard.com/) and three product pages, not Vizgard's own formal positioning statement.

## Questions left open

- Which buyer and use case does Vizgard prioritise when several are relevant?
- Which FortifAI functions are available together in a standard configuration, and which require project-specific integration?
- What is UnifAI's current availability relative to FortifAI?
- What deployment scope and measurement underpin the TRL, speed, cost and performance claims?

This is a descriptive analysis of communicated positioning. Buyer-facing quality assessment and validation of actual buyer perception remain separate.
